### Decisions

1. **`polar` is never selected on a doubt.** A missing token, a missing product id, or `POLAR_SERVER ≠ sandbox`
   all select `simulated`; `BILLING_MODE` can force `simulated` (K-BILL) but **cannot** force `polar`. The
   asymmetry is the whole safety argument for running a live checkout in a judged demo: a misconfigured
   deployment degrades to a labelled simulation rather than pointing a card form at production. `server:
   "sandbox"` is also a hard-coded literal in the client constructor, not an env read.

2. **The Billing page is a client component.** SAAS §4.6 step 4 is a polling requirement — "Confirming your
   subscription…", every 1 s for ≤ 20 s — and that *is* the demo beat: the judge returns from sandbox.polar.sh
   and the badge flips while they watch. A server component would need a manual refresh at exactly the wrong
   moment. It also means Billing renders before WP20's app shell exists, which is what let WP21·1 and WP20·2
   share the D2 evening slot without either blocking the other.

3. **Ownership is re-read from `members` before a plan row is written**, not inferred from `Principal.role`,
   in both `syncCheckout` and the simulated confirm. The principal is trustworthy; the row write is the one place
   where a wrong answer is expensive and irreversible-looking, and the extra read is one indexed query.

4. **The `[VERIFY]` fallback for Customer-State metadata is implemented, not merely noted.** `syncFromPolar`
   prefers subscriptions whose `metadata.referenceId` equals the org. If *none* carries a `referenceId` and there
   is exactly one candidate, that one is accepted (the §16 fallback). If several are unlabelled, **none** is
   chosen: guessing which org a payment belongs to is the one thing this function must never do. Both branches
   are tested.

5. **`overrides` is deliberately excluded from the sync's `onConflictDoUpdate` set.** An admin's per-org raise
   survives every Polar sync. Tested.

6. **An unrecognised product is not an upgrade.** A subscription on a product id that is neither
   `POLAR_PRODUCT_PRO` nor `POLAR_PRODUCT_BUSINESS` keeps the last known row rather than inventing a plan.

7. **`POLAR_BILLING_ENABLED` is kept as a deprecated `false`** purely so WP19's `boundaries.test.ts` — their
   file, not ours — keeps passing unmodified. `polarBillingEnabled()` is the real answer. Deletion requested in
   `docs/notes/requests/wp21-to-wp19.md`.

8. **`--legacy-peer-deps` was required and the risk was checked, not assumed.** See "Integrator actions" 3.
