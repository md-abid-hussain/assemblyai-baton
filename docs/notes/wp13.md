### Decisions

1. **lablab title is "Changeover: reps start the call, AI finishes it" (47 chars).** The TASKS-v2 wording is 52
   characters, over lablab's 50. The page `<title>` keeps the longer form.
2. **Loaded CSR cost is sourced as $28–38/h (≈ $0.47–0.63/min)**, where three independent build-ups converge
   (RDI, Office Beacon, Call Force, on the BLS OEWS median wage). That is lower than P§11's $0.60–1.00/min guess, so
   N-tail-cost uses the sourced range ($0.90 AI vs ≈ $1.41–1.89 rep for a 3-minute tail), and stays locked anyway.
3. **Market = bottom-up, US independent agencies only:** 37,000 agencies (sourced) × 500 calls/month × 12 × 25%
   policy changes × 3 min × $0.30 ≈ **$50M/year**. Every input but the agency count is an assumption and is labelled so.
   Known fabricated-looking stats (28% of CSR capacity, 6.8% error rate, 77/91/42% adoption) are listed as "do not use".
4. **The market-scan claim is "22 vendors checked"**, and the copy says 9 were only read through secondary pages.
   No hackathon-pool entries, judge names or win estimates appear anywhere public (a test scans `docs/pitch/**`,
   `src/content/**` and the types file for private research paths and those phrases).
5. **"Never re-asks" is not stated as a result.** Landing and about copy say the AI is built not to re-ask and that we
   count re-asks from the recording; the measured count comes in v2. (The PLATFORM one-liner is quoted verbatim in
   positioning.md only.)
6. **The VA-8 absolute latencies (2.84–2.89 s) are not quoted**: DESIGN marks them `reply.create`-triggered; only the
   ≈ 1.0 s difference is used.
7. **Headline numbers render only when measured.** `renderableNumbers()` drops `null` values; the landing falls back
   to "Measured on AssemblyAI's APIs while building" (61 smoke tests, 611–672 ms to `session.ready`, 650–880 ms stage
   change, 27/27 per-channel finals).
8. **Removed "licensed" from the Baton card** ("An insurance rep …"): the producer-licensing framing is unverified for
   public copy.
9. **Content types went to `src/core/contracts/ext/wp13-content.ts`** (rule 3), re-exported from `@/content`.
   `src/content` is pure (no `server-only`/`client-only`), so server and client components can both import it.
10. **Privacy (answers `wp8-to-wp13.md`):** "we delete those recordings after 7 days"; nothing claims DELETE ends a live
    session.
